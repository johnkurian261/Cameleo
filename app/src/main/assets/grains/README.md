# CAMELEO Scanned Film Grain Assets — Provenance & Licensing Notice

## 1. Provenance Statement
The film grain textures located in this directory (`app/src/main/assets/grains/`) are **externally sourced photographic research assets**.
These scans were obtained for development, architectural benchmarking, and algorithmic evaluation of silver halide emulsion rendering in the CAMELEO Android vintage camera application.

**These are NOT original assets authored by the CAMELEO team.**
**CAMELEO does not claim copyright or original authorship over these scanned film grain textures.**

## 2. Licensing and Redistribution Compliance
- **Research Status**: These assets are strictly retained within this repository under private research and educational evaluation status.
- **Pre-Release Requirement**: Prior to any public commercial release, distribution on Google Play Store, or public open-source distribution of CAMELEO, the complete licensing, origin, and redistribution rights of each scanned asset in this directory must be independently verified, or replaced with bespoke, legally licensed laboratory-scanned film emulsions (e.g. Kodak Gold 200, Tri-X 400, Ilford HP5, Portra 400).
