## 2. Historical Optical vs Film vs Scan Decomposition

1. **Lens Architecture (`OBSERVED FACT`)**:
   - Original fixed 35mm f/2.8 4-element-in-4-group Tessar-type compact lens is sharper at frame center and substantially lower in peripheral distortion/chromatic fringing than single-element plastic disposable lenses (`disp_classic`, `disp_flash`).
   - Modeled in [`PresetCatalog.kt`](file:///c:/Users/User/Downloads/DazzProject/app/src/main/java/com/craftcam/app/presets/PresetCatalog.kt#L257-L272) and [`CameraProfileCatalog.kt`](file:///c:/Users/User/Downloads/DazzProject/app/src/main/java/com/craftcam/app/camera/profile/CameraProfileCatalog.kt#L566-L573):
     - `vignetteIntensity = 0.16f`, `vignetteRadius = 0.88f` (Preset `LensConfig`) and `vignetteStrength = 0.16f`, `vignetteRadius = 0.88f` (`OpticalProfile`). Notice `ImageProcessor` sums `vigTotal = vigIntensity + lensProfile.vignetteStrength` and applies `vigMix = (vigTotal * 0.65f).coerceIn(0f, 0.55f)` (`ImageProcessor.kt:810-812`), producing restrained cos⁴ peripheral illumination falloff rather than dark disposable tunnel corners.
     - `softness = 0.030f` (`edgeSoftness = 0.030f`), preserving crisp central autofocus resolution with only slight off-axis micro-softness at f/2.8.
     - `chromaticAberration = 0.0012f`, `barrelDistortion = -0.006f` (very mild wide-angle 35mm barrel characteristic; negative sign verified against `ImageProcessor` radial warp sign convention).
     - `halation = HalationConfig(enabled = true, threshold = 0.88f, intensity = 0.14f, radius = 12f, haloRed = 1.0f, haloGreen = 0.36f, haloBlue = 0.12f)` (subtle anti-halation film backing bleed restricted to specular peaks above `0.88f`).
     - Excluded lenses: `LensType.FISHEYE_F` and `LensType.FISHEYE_W` are excluded from `MJU_II.supportedLenses` (`NORMAL`, `WIDE`, `PRISM`, `STARBURST`, `ND`, `COLOR_FLASH` only), and `d3dProfile = null`.

2. **Color Grading Without Double-Stacking (`OBSERVED FACT`)**:
   - `lut_mju2.png` is a dedicated 64×64×64 Hald-8 (`512×512` RGBA, `LutInputSpace.SRGB`, `103,288` bytes) lookup table generated by [`tools/mju_ii/calibrate_mju_ii.py`](file:///c:/Users/User/Downloads/DazzProject/tools/mju_ii/calibrate_mju_ii.py) and registered in [`generate_all_luts.py`](file:///c:/Users/User/Downloads/DazzProject/generate_all_luts.py#L685-L690).
   - `PresetCatalog.colorGrading` and `CameraProfileCatalog.MJU_II.filmProfile` hold strictly neutral multipliers (`contrast = 1.0f`, `saturation = 1.0f`, `temperature = 0.0f`, `tint = 0.0f`, `gamma = 1.0f`, `fade = 0.0f`, `toeLift = 0.0f`, `clarityStrength = 0.0f`) so color grading lives cleanly inside the LUT and film highlight shoulder (`highlightRollOff = 0.94f`, `shoulderClip = 0.992f`) with **zero duplicate contrast/saturation stacking**.
   - **Color Profile Routing (`OBSERVED FACT`)**: Mapped in [`CameraProfileCompatibility.kt`](file:///c:/Users/User/Downloads/DazzProject/app/src/main/java/com/craftcam/app/color/profile/CameraProfileCompatibility.kt#L53-L58) to `ProfilePolicy(isSupported = true, defaultProfileId = "C400")`:
     - `C400` (default): `lut_mju2.png` at 100%, `secondaryLutAsset = null`, neutral adjustment (`contrastMult = 1.0f`, etc.).
     - `EG200`, `FXN2`, `03G`: `primaryLutAsset = "lut_mju2.png"` + `secondaryLutAsset = <profile LUT>` with `dualLutMix = 0.65f` (preserving MJU II contrast backbone while adopting film-stock palette).
     - `22M`: routes strictly to monochrome `lut_profile_22m.png` (`isMonochrome = true`, zero color leaks).
